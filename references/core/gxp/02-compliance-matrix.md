---

This matrix takes 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11 (2011) and the ALCOA+ principles clause by clause and records, for each, who carries the obligation — the core, the `@bridger/gxp` companion, the regulated user's system (Consumer), or nobody (N/A) — and which Bridger identifiers **support** it. It answers [URS-BR-026](../urs.md#urs-br-026-know-which-compliance-obligations-remain-mine). The vocabulary and the honest-claims rule are in the [regulatory scope](./01-regulatory-scope.md) §5–§6.

**How to read a row.**

- **Requirement (paraphrased)** is a short paraphrase of the clause for orientation. The regulated user works from the official text.
- **Allocation** is `Core`, `Companion`, `Consumer`, `N/A`, or a `+`-joined combination. A row that names the Consumer remains the regulated user's to demonstrate, whatever else is listed.
- **Bridger identifiers** are the behaviours (`BEH-BR`), invariants (`INV-BR`) and decisions (`ADR-BR`) that support the clause or, for Consumer rows, that delimit what core does. Behaviours are defined in [behaviors/](../behaviors/01-plugin-definition.md) — the GxP hooks in [22](../behaviors/22-attributable-operations.md), [23](../behaviors/23-audit-sink-and-record.md), [24](../behaviors/24-time-ids-and-sequence.md) and [25](../behaviors/25-operation-gate.md); invariants in [invariants.md](../invariants.md); decisions in [decisions/](../decisions/028-gxp-scope.md).
- **Evidence** is `Planned (REQ-BR scenarios)` where a core behaviour is to be verified by executed acceptance scenarios and tests ([validation plan](./04-validation-plan.md)), and `—` where nothing on the library side can be evidenced. **No row has evidence today**: nothing is implemented, and a row reads *specified*, never *met* ([ADR-BR-036](../decisions/036-prospective-specification.md)).
- Every Companion allocation is a plan: the companion is specified only as a Draft, [`references/gxp/`](../../gxp/README.md) (CCR-GX-001, re-baselined against this core by CCR-GX-002), not approved and not implemented ([ADR-BR-034](../decisions/034-gxp-companion-scope.md)). Each such row links the [companion's Draft compliance matrix](../../gxp/gxp/02-compliance-matrix.md) and names the row that answers it; those answers say what the companion is specified to do and evidence nothing.

## §5 Claims this library does not make

Neither `@bridger/core` nor `@bridger/gxp` makes, and no Bridger document may make, any of the following claims. The list restates [ADR-BR-034](../decisions/034-gxp-companion-scope.md) in the form a reviewer checks documents and release notes against.

1. **Validated.** Bridger is not validated. Validation is the regulated user's activity on their computerized system in its intended use; Bridger supplies specifications and, once code exists, evidence from executed tests.
2. **Qualified.** Bridger is not qualified, and no IQ, OQ or PQ has been executed. There is no code to qualify ([ADR-BR-036](../decisions/036-prospective-specification.md)).
3. **Part 11 compliant.** Bridger makes no claim to be compliant with 21 CFR Part 11. Compliance is a property of the regulated user's system and procedures; core supports some clauses (§1) and implements none of Subpart C.
4. **Annex 11 compliant.** Bridger makes no claim to be compliant with EU GMP Annex 11, in its 2011 text or its 2025 draft.
5. **Durable trail.** Bridger does not store records and offers no durable audit trail; core keeps no copy, and durability is the responsibility of the consumer's store.
6. **Tamper-evident trail.** Bridger provides no tamper-evident trail in core. `verifyAuditSequence` detects gaps, duplicates and out-of-order numbers; anyone able to rewrite the store can renumber it, and the function will report nothing. The separate companion `@bridger/audit-chain` (Draft, not implemented) is specified to detect alteration, insertion, removal or reordering of chained records up to the last confirmed external anchor, only against a party who cannot also rewrite the anchor store; a chain without an anchor can be recomputed by anyone who can write the store, and no Bridger package calls a trail tamper-proof.
7. **Secure against hostile plugins.** Bridger is not a sandbox and gives no protection against a hostile or compromised plugin. Plugins are trusted code in the host's process; a plugin that resolves `Bridger.Host` can perform any operation the gate allows; realms change resolution only.
8. **Authentication.** Bridger does not authenticate users or bind an `Actor` to a person.
9. **Electronic signatures.** Bridger does not make any signature legally binding and provides none of the Part 11 Subpart C controls.
10. **Clock accuracy.** Bridger does not synchronise or verify clocks.
11. **Code verification.** Bridger does not verify, sign or sandbox plugin code in core; `meta.version` and `meta.digest` are labels core compares, not checks it performs. The optional `@bridger/loader` companion ([references/loader/](../../loader/README.md), Draft) is specified to verify digests and signatures before evaluation, and sandboxes nothing.
12. **Diagnostics as audit.** `Host.events` and `Host.changes` are not audit trails; both are lossy by design.
13. **Undo.** Bridger does not undo the external effects of a failed, replaced or removed plugin.
14. **Review and PQ.** Bridger provides no audit-trail review tool and no performance-qualification evidence for any user's system.
