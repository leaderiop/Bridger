Core's [compliance matrix](../../core/gxp/02-compliance-matrix.md) allocates part of several clauses of 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11 (2011) and the ALCOA+ principles to the `@bridger/gxp` companion, and until now nothing answered those allocations. This matrix answers **every** row in which core names `Companion`, one row per clause: what the companion is specified to provide, with the `BEH-GX`, `INV-GX` and `ADR-GX` identifiers that **support** the clause, and what remains the regulated user's (Consumer). It also adds rows where core allocates `Core + Consumer` but the companion is specified to add support. It answers [URS-GX-016](../urs.md#urs-gx-016-know-which-obligations-remain-mine). The vocabulary and the honest-claims rule are in the [regulatory scope](./01-regulatory-scope.md) §6–§7.

**How to read a row.**

- **Requirement (paraphrased)** is an orientation aid; the regulated user works from the official text.
- **Core allocation** repeats core's allocation for the clause. A row marked *addition* is one where core does not name the Companion.
- **Allocation** is the allocation once the companion is taken into account, in core's vocabulary.
- **Companion is specified to** names what the companion supports and the identifiers behind it. Behaviours of files 11–20 are cited by their block, for example BEH-GX-089–096 for [12 — Approval gate](../behaviors/12-approval-gate.md); the file's behaviour headings are normative.
- **Consumer remainder** is what stays the regulated user's to provide and to demonstrate, whatever the companion contributes. Core's own share is in core's matrix and is not repeated.
- **Evidence** is *Planned* in every row: no companion test exists. Requirement scenarios REQ-GX-001–047 are allocated in [traceability](../traceability.md) §5 but none is executed; the cell names the acceptance checks of [20 — Required acceptance checks](../behaviors/20-required-acceptance-checks.md) that are to supply it. No row reads *met* ([ADR-GX-026](../decisions/026-prospective-specification.md)).

## §5 Claims this companion does not make

The authoritative list is [ADR-GX-027](../decisions/027-claims-and-non-claims.md); this section restates it in the form a reviewer checks documents and release notes against. Every non-claim maps to a Consumer remainder above.

1. **Core's non-claims 1–12** of [ADR-BR-034](../../core/decisions/034-gxp-companion-scope.md) apply to the companion unchanged, including non-claim 5 as narrowed: core provides no tamper evidence, and no Bridger package prevents tampering.
2. **No statement about a whole trail.** The companion does not establish that a trail is whole or unaltered; checks report no findings of the checked kinds over a stated range, and nothing more (non-claim 13).
3. **No detection beyond the anchors.** Sealing and high-water marks detect nothing when the anchor store shares the trail's custody, and nothing after the last verified anchor; the companion cannot check custody (non-claim 14).
4. **No store property beyond the executed cases.** A conformance run does not show that a store commits what it reports stored beyond the cases executed against it (non-claim 15).
5. **No identity verification.** The companion does not verify a signer's identity, re-authentication or identification components, nor that two identifiers are two people (non-claim 16).
6. **No legal effect.** The companion does not make a signature or approval legally binding and provides no non-repudiation (non-claim 17).
7. **No secrecy against the key holder.** A fingerprint does not hide a value from whoever holds the key, nor hide a low-entropy value at all; the companion does not manage, store, back up or rotate keys (non-claim 18).
8. **No qualification.** An evidence pack does not qualify or validate a system; it is test evidence for the companion's components in one installation, and "not covered" stays not covered (non-claim 19).
9. **No retention.** The companion does not retain or archive records; an archive is a file set the user stores (non-claim 20).
10. **No procedures.** The companion does not supply or replace the user's procedures for training, access control, change control, deviations or periodic review (non-claim 21).
