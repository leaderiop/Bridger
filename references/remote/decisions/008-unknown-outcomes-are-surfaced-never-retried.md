## Context

A call whose reply is lost may or may not have run on the guest. Retrying it
automatically can repeat a side effect — the hazard the core records as
[RSK-BR-003](../../core/gxp/03-risk-assessment.md#rsk-br-003) and answers with
sticky failure ([ADR-BR-005](../../core/decisions/005-failure-is-sticky.md)).

## Decision

- Every call ends with one outcome: `executed`, `not-executed` or `unknown`,
  carried on every remote error.
- The remote package never sends a call twice, on any session.
- Late results of ended sessions are logged and dropped.

## Alternatives considered

- **Transparent retry with idempotency keys generated by the proxy.**
  Rejected: the proxy cannot know whether the guest honours them; the
  application can.

## Consequences

**Positive**: no silent duplicate side effects from the proxy.

**Negative**: callers must handle `unknown` explicitly.

**Trade-off accepted**: explicit handling over hidden repetition.
