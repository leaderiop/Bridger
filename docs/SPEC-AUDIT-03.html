Bridger spec audit, round 3: contradictions and gaps

A third semantic pass over all 15 spec trees and the user guides, made after CCR-BR-034 and its companion records closed round 2. It looks for new problems and for round-2 fixes that were applied in one place but not carried to every place they reach. The mechanical verifiers pass; everything here is a problem of meaning they cannot see.

How to read thisEach finding is tagged contradiction (two normative statements that cannot both hold), gap (a case the spec leaves undefined) or regression (a round-2 fix that is incomplete or opened a new hole). verified means the coordinator re-read both sides in the source and the finding held; the other findings are agent-reported with quoted evidence and should be confirmed when fixed. 02 · Patterns

Most findings trace back to five root causes. Fixing the cause closes several findings at once.

Round-2 fixes not carried throughThe largest group. The shutdown/WouldDeadlock rule (C6), dormant and "refused-digest" in chapter 07 (C3/H3), the force-revocation lists (C5), the parked-child resume list (C8), the correlationId exceptions (H1), expect without a digest (H2), "creating" family members (C16), the minVersion backlog (P4), received shred notices (P12) and the binding-v2 signer text (H5) were each fixed in one place and left stale in the others.
Rules that need a port operation that does not existOutboxStore cannot record an incarnation; GuestTransport has no profile; HashChainOptions has no key; SessionStore cannot create a session; no core seam clears the principal; nothing connects the MCP server to McpTaskBridge; KeyWrapper is named but never declared.
Principals not wiredThe agent loop never runs as its own plugin, command wrappers cannot drop the caller's principal, Entitlement.guard has no route to the service, shred acknowledgements take host from the payload, and the install GapRecord is unattributed.
Keyed chains cannot be builtA KeyedHasher cannot reach the sink, verification needs a pure synchronous MAC while custody is a hardware module, and verifyBundle has one expectKeyed for many series.
Guarantees stated wider than the designINV-BR-043 ("what an approver reviewed is what runs") holds only when meta.digest is declared; GxP sealing "detects removal" but never reads receipts above the stored head; mutation evidence can never count the OQ suite.
03 · High severity

H1The shutdown WouldDeadlock rule cannot be decided, and contradicts the record and join rules regressionverified

Where
core/behaviors/12-shutdown-and-hosting.md:205-218 vs 09-leases.md:503-524, decisions/016-reentrancy-detection.md:31-33, invariants.md:553-556 (INV-BR-016), :773 (INV-BR-022), 08:131-136, 22:712-717, 22:748-753, 12:426
Evidence
12 (round-2 fix C6): a shutdown from a build, load or finalizer fiber "MUST fail at once with WouldDeadlock … MUST NOT begin shutdown or join one in progress, and MUST write no record", but "a lease callback's shutdown therefore does not fail". ADR-016 and BEH-BR-088 put lease holders and build/finalizer fibers in the same HeldPlugins set, and no rule marks load fibers, so the host cannot tell the allowed case from the refused one. INV-BR-016 read literally refuses the lease callback too. 08 and 22 say every WouldDeadlock is decided after the requested record and "always recorded"; INV-BR-022 requires a record pair for every call that passes attribution, latch and gate; 22:716 and BEH-BR-109 make a shutdown during a shutdown join it. Found independently by three reviews.
Fix
Add a separate fiber reference (for example CurrentTransition) set only by build, load and finalizer fibers; amend ADR-016, BEH-BR-088 and INV-BR-016. Then write the shutdown check as an explicit step before step 6 (no record, no join) into BEH-BR-188, BEH-BR-198, INV-BR-022 and BEH-BR-109, or record it like every other WouldDeadlock.

H2The shutdown error union and guarded-call lists were updated in two places only regressionverified

Where
core/behaviors/02:341, 12:35 (updated) vs 02:535-537 (BEH-BR-014), 12:519 (BEH-BR-110), 22:190-191, 22:209 (BEH-BR-186), 09:517-524 (BEH-BR-088), decisions/008-error-taxonomy.md:44
Evidence
The new union is Unattributed | ReasonRequired | WouldDeadlock; the stale copies still read "Unattributed | ReasonRequired" or "fails only with Unattributed or ReasonRequired". BEH-BR-013 (02:406-412) says a differing copy is a defect. ADR-008's table "lists every error core defines" with its raisers, and its WouldDeadlock row omits shutdown, which undoes c3fc08d's claim (C23) that the table is complete.
Fix
Add WouldDeadlock to all four places, and shutdown to BEH-BR-088, INV-BR-016's Source and ADR-008's row.

H3A subagent's approval can never be resumed contradictionverified

Where
agent/behaviors/04-subagents.md:251-253, :81-93 vs 02-tasks.md:224-229, 01-agent-seams.md:181-199
Evidence
04: a subagent's awaiting-approval is reflected in the parent, whose "step waits". 02: on a deferral the loop "MUST end the current step … and MUST hold no pinned toolset, model call or budget reservation while it waits"; 01: a turn ends at a deferral and the pin is released. 04: the subagent realm is removed when "the parent turn ends" and "MUST NOT outlive its parent turn", failing with parent-ended. The draft is orphaned. adf1bdf's rule that every subagent turn uses spawn.toolset ties it to the pin released at that same moment.
Fix
Either keep the parent turn (pin and scope) open while a subagent waits, or end the subagent on deferral and let the parent re-spawn after resume; say which task the application resumes.

H4The agent loop never runs under its own principal, so PlanAffectsCaller can miss it regression

Where
agent/behaviors/01-agent-seams.md:143, :368-372, :405-410; mcp/behaviors/04-host-tools.md:293-296 vs core/behaviors/28-plugin-principal-and-mandatory-policy.md:218-229
Evidence
Agent: dispatch's onBehalfOf "names a plugin — the loop's, since a service method runs under its caller's principal"; host tools refuse with PlanAffectsCaller "because the dispatch's onBehalfOf names the loop's plugin". But AgentLoop.start is itself a service method, and core says a service method "runs under the caller's principal, or none for application code". Nothing makes the turn run under the loop's principal (no background, no asSelf), so a plan that stops the loop plugin passes the check.
Fix
Require each turn to run under the loop's own principal (Plugin.Runtime.background or asSelf), or name the loop in the call path another way.

H5Command wrappers and admissions must run with "no plugin principal", which a companion cannot arrange contradictionverified

Where
command/behaviors/06-receipts-wrappers-and-breakers.md:388-391, :569-571 vs core/behaviors/28:106-111, :225-229; core/31:912
Evidence
Command: wrappers and each admit "MUST run in the dispatching fiber … and with no plugin principal". Core: the principal lives in "a fiber reference whose key @bridger/core does not export for providing". An MCP dispatch or a nested dispatch from a handler carries a principal and the command package has no API to clear it; only core's own hook dispatch can.
Fix
Add a core seam that runs an effect with no principal, or let wrappers and admissions run under the dispatcher's principal and say so.

H6GxP verifySeals never checks anchored windows above a cut-off tail regressionverified

Where
gxp/behaviors/21-sealing-through-audit-chain.md:152-155 vs 21:269-272, audit-chain/behaviors/03-verification.md:101
Evidence
21 (round-2 fix H4/P14): read receipts with sealing.receipts.read(hostId, 1, s), "s the highest auditSeq read from the store". If the newest entries are deleted but their receipts survive, those statements fall outside [1, s] and are never confirmed; audit-chain's "window-not-in-entries" kind exists for exactly this. 21 promises it "detects … removal … of sealed records up to the last verified anchor". The high-water marks may be "not-used".
Fix
Read receipts up to the highest toSeq the receipt reader holds (or Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER), not the store's head.

H7A binding field must be "never empty", but core always sends mandatoryInterceptions, often as [] contradictionverified

Where
gxp/behaviors/09-approval-tickets.md:98-104, 11-signature-linking.md:135 vs core/behaviors/25-operation-gate.md:214-217, :190-194
Evidence
GxP: allowChildren and mandatoryInterceptions "whenever the request will carry them … A field that does not apply MUST be absent, never null or empty". Core: they "MUST be present for every plugin-addressed operation". GxP 11 copies them "as given". An issuer that drops [] and a gate that copies it compute different digests, so every plugin-addressed call with no interceptions is refused as binding-mismatch. definition: {} has the same problem. No test vector has an empty array.
Fix
State that an empty array or object the request carries is bound as given (or always omitted, on both sides), and add a vector.

H8GuestTransport has no profile, yet guest-side rules read its liveness and residency gapverified

Where
remote/behaviors/07-transports-and-adapters.md:42-44, :282-286 vs 09:183-186, 05:122, 09:349-357, 08:303 (check 36)
Evidence
GuestTransport is only { accept }; only Transport has profile. MultiplexedGuest.endpoint(name) takes no residency, unlike Multiplexed.endpoint. Yet Guest.serve on a "per-call" transport MUST be given a DedupeStore or fail to start, the guest MUST keep no session table, and placement is evaluated with "the labels of their own GuestTransport profile". Federation exports served over multiplexed endpoints can never carry labels.
Fix
Add readonly profile: TransportProfile to GuestTransport and options.residency to MultiplexedGuest.endpoint.

H9The raised incarnation must be recorded, but no port operation can write it regressionverified

Where
durable-bus/behaviors/06:355-381, 01:205-207 vs 02:153-168 (OutboxStore), 03:80-100 (InboxStore), 06:424-426
Evidence
Round-2 fix H8: "then run; then record the raised incarnation in the store run left", reporting origin-unrecorded while it cannot. OutboxStore has only readonly incarnation: Effect<number, StoreUnavailable>, a read. The recorded value is not keyed by node, and the replacement store "comes from another host", so a copied store recording that host's higher number triggers the start refusal of 01:205-207.
Fix
Add recordIncarnation(tx, node, n) to OutboxStore, key the recorded incarnation by node, and say what start does when the store records another node's number.

07 · Method and limits

Eight read-only reviews ran in parallel with the same split as round 2: core behaviours 01–12, 13–24 and 25–34; the rest of core (ADRs, invariants, URS, risk, validation, process); the GxP companion against core and audit-chain; loader, kit, command, mcp and agent; remote, federation, durable-bus, event-store and sequence; audit-chain, entitlement, keyring and the guides. Each was told to look for new contradictions and gaps and for incomplete round-2 fixes (reading the diffs of adf1bdf and c3fc08d), to skip what the verifiers check and anything already listed as a known gap, and to quote both sides at file:line. Findings reported by more than one review (the shutdown rule, the hook correlationId, the plan cycle, ADR-018) are merged. The coordinator re-read 16 findings in the source; all held.
