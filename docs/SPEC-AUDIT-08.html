02 · Patterns

Most findings trace back to four causes.

Follow-up rules decided against one caseThe take-over of a draining stop assumed a remove (H1); reverify was checked for manifest verification but not for loading and gate installs after a restart (H2); the hold pauses subscriptions but not direct reads (H3); refusing settle under origin-unrecorded assumed that hold lifts on its own (H4); clearing pins on stop assumed no kill-switch run follows (L1).
Ownership of a child stop still has gapsSystem stops and shutdown's stop of a child whose parent is still building have no owner (C2); the resumer lists lack the new "before begun" limit (C1); cleanup failure (C3), revocation of a background fiber (C4) and how a scoped lease reaches HeldPlugins (C5) are undefined.
The subagent hand-off has no trigger and no normal endingNothing says how the model asks for a subagent (A2), how a done/failed/needs-input subagent settles the result (A3), how the parent learns of the settlement (A4), or who is named when the host ends it (A5).
Propagation, as every roundThe cold handoff exception in 34, REQ-BR-088, ADR-049 and 08 (C6); glossary and ADR-014 (CL7, CL8); traceability risk rows (CL6); core risk entries for the loader (PL15); durable-bus overview and catalogs (PL20); keyring traceability (PL21).
03 · High severity

H1Taking over a draining outside stop is defined only for remove; for an operator restart, replace or reconfigure its MUSTs conflict contradictionverified

Where
core/behaviors/14-children-and-groups.md:150-168, :117-127; 04-states-and-snapshots.md:241-245; 21-testing-host.md:1197-1202 (check 89 tests only an unforced remove)
Evidence
14: the take-over "MUST NOT join, supersede, redirect or cancel the operator operation that owns the stop, which continues after its step … Once the step has settled, a child still registered MUST be removed by the removal itself"; 04 keeps the stop's reason and next. After the stop step a restart/replace/reconfigure builds again, so the child can be removed only by superseding that operation; waiting instead is bounded by buildTimeout, not the promised drainTimeout plus revocation.
Fix
Decide one outcome: the take-over ends the operator operation like a parked intent (intent-ended, reason "remove", next "gone"), or it waits for the build and states that buildTimeout bounds the wait. Add restart and replace cases to check 89.

H2Round-7 L3 ("an offline host past maxAge boots on what it accepted") fails at the first restart: every load and gate install is a new acceptance contradictionverified

Where
federation/behaviors/01-host-manifest.md:179-186 (BEH-FD-002), README.md:183, 05-testing-and-acceptance.md:90 vs loader/behaviors/02-load-pipeline.md:568-576, 04-revocation-and-kill-switch.md:302-306, federation/01:353-362 (BEH-FD-005), 05:116 (check 28)
Evidence
BEH-FD-002: reverify: { acceptedAt } is there "so that a host offline longer than the loader's maxAge … still applies what it accepted". Loader: older than maxAge the gate "MUST refuse install and replace with reason … "revocation-stale"" (admission (c) not exempt), and a load "MUST fail with RevocationStale"; after a restart "the plugin holds no resolved definition". Check 28 already expects such installs refused. The manifest verifies; none of its entries runs.
Fix
Give the resolver and LoaderGate a reverify-style restore for the digests of the stored highest manifest, or reword BEH-FD-002, BEH-FD-005, check 2 and CCR-FD-010: the manifest applies but its entries stay pending until a fresh set arrives.

H3During apply-pending and the install window, EventStore.read decodes a held subject in plaintext, though the keyring says no subject reopens contradictionverified

Where
keyring/behaviors/03-shred-and-acceptance.md:447-449, :468, :492, :679; keyring/invariants.md:63-66; keyring/behaviors/02-field-encryption.md (BEH-KR-012) vs durable-bus/behaviors/06-replay-and-snapshots.md:452-456; event-store/behaviors/05-storage-port-and-acceptance.md:267-268
Evidence
Keyring: "no subject is reopened meanwhile"; INV-KR-002 (c) "reopens no subject, since durable-bus holds every reader"; check 10 "no decode served on the new store before it commits". Durable-bus holds only "every subscription and every follow". The codec yields Shredded only from a tombstone in the KeyStore (round 7 removed the shared state), and EventStore.read/readAll decode through it with nothing pausing them.
Fix
Have the codec consult the participant's held set through the shared KeyringPorts (held subject → Shredded), or narrow "every reader paused" and "no decode served" to subscriptions and follow and add event-store reads to the accepted residuals.

H4settle is refused while origin-unrecorded holds, but nothing lifts origin-unrecorded, so federation's recovery is blocked until restart regressionverified

Where
durable-bus/behaviors/06-replay-and-snapshots.md:470-476 vs :504-509; federation/behaviors/02-sync-offline-and-rebootstrap.md:471, :478-482; federation/05:134 (check 46); docs/guides/error-catalog.md:1559
Evidence
The second follow-up made settle fail "store-swap-pending" while origin-unrecorded holds. No rule retries recordIncarnation (grep: none). Before, a retried install raised again and recorded into a fresh store — the only way out while the process ran. Federation still says "install runs again over it" and the catalog "the re-bootstrap retries on its own". A transient record failure now keeps writes refused and readers paused until the process restarts.
Fix
Have the bus retry recordIncarnation under bounded backoff on its background fiber, as for apply; or let settle run when only origin-unrecorded holds (a new raise keeps origins unique). State the step-1 block in BEH-FD-014 and check 46.
