Report 03 · Cordis lens

Temporal composability, scopes, contributions and cleanup

Bridger's cleanup model (one forked Effect Scope per activation, a ContributionRegistry for shared state, dependency-ordered stops, sticky failed(cleanup)) is stricter and better specified than Cordis's. The largest real gap is liveness: nothing bounds a hung finalizer in the activation scope (INV-BR-030) or in a revoked registry holder (BEH-BR-167). Failures are reported as one squashed message, there is no resource-level diagnostic tree, and nothing lets a plugin mark an effect irreversible or withhold build-time emissions until publication. Most fixes are additive diagnostics plus one new timeout; none change the lifecycle state machine.

Scope: references/core behaviours 05, 06, 13, 19; command BEH-CM-050; prototypes/host-core/src/{host,registry}.ts

Not checked / uncertainties

  • Checked: Scope.close has no implicit bound and a timeout over it hangs on a hung finalizer (probe); a finalizer added to an already closed scope runs at once; several failing finalizers surface in LIFO order in the cause.
  • Checked: shutdown (12) bounded the whole shutdown only, never a single plugin's close; T1 stood as written. BEH-BR-107 was amended.
  • Checked: no rule withheld build-time emissions (05, 16); T6 stood.
  • The prototype was run: every change has a test and a mutation check (breaking the code failed the test).
  • Not done: retryCleanup cannot retry a finalizer not acquired through acquire, because a Layer hides its finalizers in closures of nested scopes that the host cannot reach (probed: wrapping every reachable finalizer captured only the labelled release, and Effect's scopeClose on an already closed scope does nothing, so a composite closure cannot be re-run); a retry also runs a release a second time, which Cordis forbids (at most once), so a kept release must be safe to repeat; Plugin.compensate stays unplanned; the per-plugin closeTimeout lives on the plugin definition, not the manifest.
  • Cordis comparisons were checked against the paper (arXiv 2608.25512): inertia (§4.4), the failure outcome that withholds re-entry (§4.4), awaiting `fiber.dispose()` with no bound (Algorithm, §5.1.3), recovery at most once (§5.1.1) and acquisition versus emission with withholding and compensation (§6.1) are stated there. `getEffects` and `INACTIVE_EFFECT` come from the Cordis fiber documentation, not the paper. One row was wrong and is corrected: Cordis does not "re-run" after a failure. The animated diagrams were re-read against the as-built behaviour: the close-timeout cards said the closing fiber is interrupted, which is wrong (a finalizer cannot be interrupted); they now say the wait ends and the close keeps running detached. They remain schematic, not generated from the spec.
