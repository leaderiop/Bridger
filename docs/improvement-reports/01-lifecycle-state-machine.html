Improvement report 01 · Bridger spec and prototype review

Lifecycle and state machine

Bridger's lifecycle is already stricter than Cordis: a total operation table, per-plugin linearization, cycle rejection at install, bounded drains and atomic reconcile with undo. The largest real defect is a spec/prototype divergence on stale builds, then a confluence guarantee weaker than Cordis' theorem, no activation identity, and missing operator controls (scoped settle, abort of a parked stop, activation rollback, event correlation).

2 High6 Medium3 Low11 findings · 10 recommendations
  1. Total 8x8 legality, per-plugin linearization, install-time cycle rejection, bounded drains, atomic reconcile with undo.
  2. BEH-BR-039 records a stale build that failed after provider loss as failed; the prototype (host.ts:1125) silently discards it, and no test covers it.
  3. Confluence (BEH-BR-160) carves out failure, lazy wake, refusal and handoff, and the property tests never inject failure.
  4. No never-reused activation identity: replacement identity is (id, realm) only.
  5. No scoped quiescence, no cancel for a parked stop, no activation-level rollback; events cannot be joined to snapshot revisions or causes.
