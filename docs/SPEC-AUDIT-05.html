02 · Patterns

Most findings trace back to four causes.

Round-4 fixes not carried throughThe largest group again, and two of them now contradict their own fix. H1 added a "wait, never revoke" rule next to the older "resume and revoke" rule for the same parked stop. H3's scoped fence changed publish but not the write helpers that call it (H2). The canonical key JSON.stringify([realm, id]) reached the re-entrancy references and nothing else (C2 and many lows). The "^" + V range (F4), the four audit-loss paths (C6), the whenAvailable consult point and chainLinkUnchecked stopped short too.
Rules that need a mechanism nobody declaredTasks has no write side and no route from cancel to the loop running the task (H3). AcceptedDigests has no writers (L2). Verification records lack the signer identity that revocation needs (H7). Core has no cold option on replace (H9). readArchive takes no receipt verifiers (H10). auditPosition has no highest-assigned number (C5). The import proxy has no transport profile before dialling (H5).
Federation re-bootstrap retryRound 4's F2/F3 fix opened new holes. The delivery imports that step 1 reopens cannot open while their link is suspended (H4). The marker forgets that an install began (H6). Step 1's offer is forbidden on a suspended link (F1). A step-2 failure resumes links that a retry must keep suspended (F2).
The shutdown tailRecords lost or written after the close report have no channel. The latch publishes after the final snapshot and after closed (C3). The lost-record log carries a correlationId where only three places may (C4). Close-flush losses have no number to compare against (C5). GxP's recordClose reads before the flush ends (G4).
03 · High severity

H1Round 4's H1 fix gives a parked child stop two outcomes: wait without revoking, or resume and revoke contradictionregressionverified

Where
core/behaviors/14-children-and-groups.md:77-89, :440-446, 09-leases.md:441-447 vs 14:95-104, 09-leases.md:417-431, 08-operation-legality.md:746-754, invariants.md:330-334 (INV-BR-009), :646-650 (INV-BR-017); check 89 at 21-testing-host.md:1146-1152 asserts both
Evidence
14 (round-4 fix): the finalizer's wait "MUST follow the limits of the parent's stop … MUST NOT revoke a lease that call did not force — a drain that expires without the call's force: true parks the child's stop with the call … and the wait continues until the parked stop is resumed". But 14:95: "When the child's stop is parked as its sub-scope closes or Plugin.Runtime.unmount is called for it … the removal MUST resume that stop … MUST revoke at the limit and never park again". 08:752 (also tagged CCR-BR-036): "a sub-scope close or an unmount MUST resume it under BridgerOptions.drainTimeout, revoking on expiry". 09 lists a sub-scope close or unmount "that itself removes a child or resumes its parked stop" as a path with no caller. So once an operator's unforced remove of the parent parks the child's stop, the sub-scope close resumes it and revokes leases the operator never forced, which is what H1 was meant to stop. Two more claims in 14 are now false: 14:65-66 says the wait is "bounded by drainTimeout … then by the revocation", but it is now unbounded; and 14:69-74 has a lease held by the closing fiber "revoked at the limit", which under H1 becomes a self-wait with no WouldDeadlock. Found by two reviews.
Fix
A stop parked by the parent's own stop stays the parent's: a sub-scope close or unmount only waits for it. Limit the resume-and-revoke rule (14:95, 08:746/753/1241, 09:421, INV-BR-009/017, check 89) to stops parked outside the parent's stop. State what happens to a lease the closing fiber holds itself.

H2The scoped fence did not reach the write helpers, so a write admitted before a freeze can still commit after it regressionverified

Where
durable-bus/behaviors/02-transactional-outbox.md:46, 06-replay-and-snapshots.md:452-458, event-store/behaviors/05-storage-port-and-acceptance.md:123-128 vs event-store/behaviors/01-streams-and-append.md:156, 02-decide-and-evolve.md:92,152, 05:247, sequence/behaviors/01-series-and-ownership.md:89-106, keyring/behaviors/01-keys-and-subjects.md:112, federation/behaviors/02-sync-offline-and-rebootstrap.md:344
Evidence
publish now needs StoreTransaction | Scope.Scope, and a fence "is admitted for the Scope it runs in, which MUST be the transaction's". But Stream.append, Aggregate.handle/record, EventStoreShape.append, sequence next/open/close still require only StoreTransaction, and KeyStore.transaction excludes only StoreTransaction. Federation's type InstallContext = StoreTransaction is the same (coordinator check). An implementation that keeps these types has to open its own Scope inside the call, so the admission ends when append returns, before the commit. That is exactly the window H3 closed.
Fix
Add Scope.Scope to the requirements of every write helper, EventStoreShape.append, the SequenceShape members and Series, and InstallContext. Make KeyStore.transaction provide and exclude the transaction's Scope, as InboxStore.transaction does. Check command's receiptContext (command/06:193) for the same gap.

H3Tasks is a base service with no write side, and nothing routes Tasks.cancel to the loop running the task gapverified

Where
agent/behaviors/02-tasks.md:158-172, :182-189 vs 01-agent-seams.md:228, :470, 02-tasks.md:135, agent/invariants.md:100 (INV-AG-004), 04-subagents.md:116
Evidence
The Tasks shape has only get, list, changes, all, await, cancel. Yet "The loop MUST report every state change of a task through Tasks", and an application "MAY replace either with its own provider". INV-AG-004 calls Tasks.ts "the only writer of task state". BEH-AG-010: "cancel MUST interrupt the running turn, await its finalizers". But the turn runs in whichever AgentLoop started it, which may be a subagent's loop in an ephemeral realm, and Tasks.cancel (used by the MCP bridge and spawnSubagent) has no way to reach it.
Fix
Declare the write side (Tasks.create/publish, or a separate TaskWriter) and a per-task cancel handle the owning loop registers. State that Tasks.cancel and AgentLoop.cancel both go through it.

H4A retry's step 1 reopens delivery imports that cannot open while their link is suspended contradictionverified

Where
federation/behaviors/02-sync-offline-and-rebootstrap.md:366-374, :470-473 vs 04-exports-and-imports.md:125-131, :142-145 (BEH-FD-026); check 31 (05:118)
Evidence
Step 1, on an attempt that starts with links suspended, "MUST first reopen the endpoints of the imports named in FederationOptions.deliveryImports", and every link "MUST show suspended … through every retry". But the proxy's open returns a connection "only while that link is up … otherwise open MUST fail at once", and "While the link is not up … the proxy MUST provide nothing". Let open succeed for a deliveryImports entry during step 1 while its link is suspended, or add a PeerLinkStatus state for it. Amend BEH-FD-026 and check 31.

H5The import proxy is built before any link exists, so the dialer-profile residency fallback cannot reach its placement check gap

Where
federation/behaviors/04:125-131 (BEH-FD-026), 06:300-305, 06:320-327, 03:436-439 vs remote/behaviors/09:280-290 (BEH-RM-068)
Evidence
BEH-FD-045 offers the route the ManifestPeer residency "or, absent that, the residency of the profile the LinkDialer … yielded". Remote.define "MUST evaluate the policy against transport.profile.residency", and the build fails with PlacementRefused. BEH-FD-026 never says what the proxy transport's profile is, and the apply order installs proxies in step (3) and dials in step (6). An import with residency whose labels come only from the dialer fails its build, and the failure is sticky (BEH-FD-032). Check 37 cannot pass for imports.
Fix
Declare the federation endpoint transport's profile with residency known before dialling (for example LinkDialer.profile(peer)). Otherwise require ManifestPeer.residency for any import that carries a residency, and narrow check 37.

H6The re-bootstrap marker forgets a failed install, so a crash during the retry can resume links over half-replaced state gapverified

Where
federation/behaviors/06-kill-path-placement-and-skew.md:503, :538-555, 02:358-360 vs federation/invariants.md:216-219 (INV-FD-011), 06:563-565
Evidence
Steps are "required" | "settled" | "fetched" | "installing" | "installed", each stored "before acting on it". At activation only "a marker at "installing" or "installed" MUST keep every link suspended". A retry after a failed step 3a runs from step 1 and rewrites the marker to "settled" or "fetched". A crash then has no rule that keeps links suspended, although "local state may be half replaced", which breaks INV-FD-011.
Fix
Persist an install-attempted flag in RebootstrapMarker, or never lower the step below "installing" once an install has begun. Define the restart behaviour for every step value.

H7Signer re-evaluation is required in three places that never receive the signer identities it needs gapverified

Where
loader/behaviors/09-trust-class-and-guest-links.md:479, :487-490, 04-revocation-and-kill-switch.md:327-332, :513, 02-load-pipeline.md:336, :447 vs 03-signature-policy.md:104, :169-172
Evidence
RevokedDigests.layer, filterDesired and LoaderGate must compute "by signer through the re-evaluation of BEH-LD-030": "the recorded verified signers minus every signer whose identity the set revokes". But VerificationRecord holds only signerId: string // the TrustedSigner.id. The identity lives on the policy's TrustedSigner, and none of the three is given the policy. Records of a replaced loader are kept "for revocation" after its policy is gone. The kill switch must pass "the rule's pinned digests", but no accessor for them exists.
Fix
Record each verified signer's identity, and the rule that was met, in VerificationRecord. Alternatively, pass the rules by policyId to the three consumers and say how a replaced loader's policies are kept.

H8The revocation reconcile leaves out installed entries that are still pending, so core prunes them under the revocation actor contradictionverified

Where
loader/behaviors/04-revocation-and-kill-switch.md:440-453 vs core/behaviors/18-reconciliation-and-confluence.md:328-332; INV-LD-005
Evidence
Loader: "an entry still in status.pending, or removed by hand, MUST NOT be in the list". Pending covers refused, parked, drain-timeout, latch and held entries, and a refused replace or a parked stop leaves the plugin installed and owned by the scope. Core: "With prune … every plugin owned by the scope and absent from desired MUST be removed". So the reconcile removes plugins whose digests were never revoked, which breaks "its only actions are the prune removals of the dropped entries".
Fix
Keep every entry installed in Host.snapshot in the list, as its last completed action left it, whatever its pending status. Leave out only entries absent from the snapshot.

H9bridger dev --cold passes a cold option that core does not offer on replace or reconcile gapverified

Where
kit/behaviors/04-cli.md:120-125, check 27 (kit/behaviors/08-required-acceptance-checks.md:115) vs core/behaviors/02-host-construction.md:390-394, 18-reconciliation-and-confluence.md:64-71, 34-state-handoff.md (BEH-BR-353)

H10An archive's seal check can never confirm an anchor, and it cannot report a head mismatch gapverified

Where
gxp/behaviors/18-archive-and-export.md:232-262 vs audit-chain/behaviors/05-verification-bundle.md:162-164, :198-203, BEH-AC-036; compare gxp/behaviors/21-sealing-through-audit-chain.md:172
Evidence
readArchive takes only scope, policy, pageSize, maxOpenRequests, maxEntries and keyed, and calls verifyBundle with keyed only. verifyBundle takes receiptVerifiers? and "MUST report every other receipt as "not-checked" or "failed"", and audit-chain's main entry "MUST export no offline verifier". So every archived receipt becomes anchorUnconfirmed and verifiedThrough stays 0. The archive's own high-water marks are never passed as expectedHeads (verifySeals does pass them), so headMismatch cannot arise, yet it is not listed as not checked.
Fix
Add receiptVerifiers, pinnedTrustRoots and expected heads (from the archived high-water marks) to readArchive and pass them on. Otherwise list headMismatch as not checked and state that archived anchors stay unconfirmed.
